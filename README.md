# LexiToons

**A Bilingual Comic-First Cognitive Development Platform for Dyslexic Children**

LexiToons is a cross-platform mobile application designed to aid dyslexic children aged 6–12 in Pakistan through visual storytelling, multisensory gamification, and AI-assisted bilingual learning (English & Urdu).

---

## 📌 Problem & Solution

* **Problem:** Existing dyslexia tools rely heavily on dense text and lack bilingual support, leading to poor engagement among young learners. Furthermore, no existing platform addresses the specific linguistic and cultural context of Pakistan, where an estimated 12 million children are affected by dyslexia.
* **Solution:** LexiToons delivers a visual-first, gamified experience built specifically for Pakistani dyslexic learners, blending interactive comic narratives with AI-powered assessment and progress analytics.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Bilingual Mobile Application:** Built with Flutter for seamless cross-platform performance on both Android and iOS in English and Urdu.
* **Comic-Based Learning:** Animated visual stories and interactive, multisensory gamified learning modules tailored to cognitive development.
* **AI Screening Engine:** Utilizes bilingual NLP and speech recognition to enable early dyslexia detection and personalized learning pathways.
* **Parent & Therapist Dashboard:** Visual progress tracking, detailed analytics reporting, and module management tools for guardians and specialists.

---

## 👥 Target Users

* **Children (Ages 6–12):** Engage directly with interactive comic narratives and educational mini-games.
* **Parents & Guardians:** Monitor real-time cognitive growth and skill mastery via visual dashboards.
* **Therapists:** Oversee multiple students, manage intervention modules, and export analytical progress reports.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Domains

* **Mobile App Development:** Flutter (Dart) — Android & iOS
* **AI & Machine Learning:** NLP (Natural Language Processing), Speech Recognition
* **Analytics:** Dashboard & Data Visualization
* **Domains:** Special Education, Dyslexia Intervention, Gamification, Edutainment
